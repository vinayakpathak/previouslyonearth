# Argentina diary

Where you have been on the trip, in your own words, with the newest day at the end. Claude reads this before suggesting places, so as not to suggest one twice. Its suggestions are in [suggestions.md](suggestions.md).

## Before 26 November 2026: Güerrín

The pizza was decent. Incredibly fatty. Not something I want to go to again and again.

## Thursday 26 November 2026: Plaza de Mayo and La Ideal

We went to Plaza de Mayo at 3:30 and we saw the Madres march. It gave me goosebumps and I also felt emotional to know that those women had their kids kidnapped and they never found them. We marched with the Madres for a bit. This was really the highlight of the day. I felt quite some solidarity with them for some reason, even though I don't really have much to do with their movement.

Also, looking at Casa Rosada and imagining Eva Perón giving her iconic speeches from there was amazing.

We also then walked on the diagonal road to La Ideal. The cafe La Ideal was very palatial, I don't know why. It was also overpriced. It was an OK experience.

## Friday 27 November 2026: Dinner at La Aguada

They suggested two dishes that they said were good and traditional. One was a steak simmered in milk (matambre a la leche). They said it becomes really soft. But it was quite a disappointment. It came with stuff that looked like milk curds. I mean, the taste was OK. It was garlicky. But still, the thought of eating it with milk curds was quite gross.

They also recommended this veggie dish, which looked like a mix of many different kinds of veggies, but then they added loads of cheese on top. And the veggies weren't even well seasoned. So overall a pretty disappointing dinner.
