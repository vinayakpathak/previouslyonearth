# Reading patterns

What the reading record in `books.yml` shows about how you read, so that every
recommendation starts from the same picture. Claude updates this whenever a
book is logged. Each point names the books it rests on, by their keys in
`books.yml`, so it can be checked and corrected. Edit or delete anything you
disagree with.

## What works

- **The benchmark is *A Flower Traveled in My Blood*:** a big history told
  through one family and real people, emotionally vivid, perfectly paced and
  well ordered. *(gilliland)*
- **Travel memoir with history mixed in:** a writer living in or travelling
  through a place and teaching its history along the way. Three for three so
  far. *(france, durrell, winter)*
- **A few real people, written by someone who got to know them.**
  *(guerriero, gilliland)*
- **Humour keeps you reading.** *(durrell)*

## What doesn't

- **Material beyond what you came for:** a long tangent, or every claim
  weighed from all angles. *(timerman, hedges)*
- **Repetition and padding, not length.** Bridges felt twice as long as it
  needed to be, but Gilliland, at about 470 pages, was hard to put down.
  *(bridges, gilliland)*
- **Academic, exhaustive treatments,** even on a subject you chose.
  *(hedges)*
- **Many characters with unfamiliar names over many pages** are hard to keep
  track of. Self-contained episodes, and reminders of what came long before,
  make a book easier. *(bridges, hudson)*
- **Slow starts:** Winter took a third of the book to win you over. *(winter)*

## How you move between books

- **You follow threads.** France's mentions of Eva Perón led to Hedges, and an
  interest in Argentina's animals led to Durrell. The dictatorship runs
  through France, Timerman and Gilliland. Pick the next book on a thread in a
  form you enjoy, not just the most authoritative one. *(france, hedges,
  durrell, timerman, gilliland)*
- **After an intense book, you want something lighter.** *(hudson after
  gilliland)*

## Not yet known

- **How hard is too hard?** Nothing so far was rated hard, and most
  difficulty ratings are Claude's guesses. Only Bridges (moderate) and Hudson
  (easy) are your own.
