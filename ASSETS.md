# Asset sources

All visual assets and typefaces are stored locally. Visiting the website does not require requests to Google Fonts or the Metropolitan Museum of Art.

## Typefaces

- **Cormorant Garamond** — by the Cormorant Project Authors. Latin variable WOFF2, weights **300–700**, normal and italic. Downloaded from Google Fonts. Files: `src/assets/fonts/cormorant-garamond-regular.woff2` and `src/assets/fonts/cormorant-garamond-italic.woff2`.
- **Inter** — by the Inter Project Authors. Latin variable WOFF2, weights **100–900**, normal. Downloaded from Google Fonts. File: `src/assets/fonts/inter-regular.woff2`.

Both families are licensed under the **SIL Open Font License 1.1**. Their full licenses are included alongside the font files as `Cormorant-Garamond-OFL.txt` and `Inter-OFL.txt`, obtained from the official [Cormorant Garamond](https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/cormorantgaramond) and [Inter](https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/inter) directories in Google Fonts.

The downloaded Latin subsets include English, common Western European accented characters, and standard punctuation. Additional language subsets may be needed for writing in other scripts.

## Historical artwork

**Giovanni Battista Piranesi**, *The Forum Romanum, or Campo Vaccino, from the Capitol* (*Veduta di Campo Vaccino*), ca. **1775**. Etching from *Vedute di Roma (Views of Rome)*.

- File: `src/assets/images/roman-forum.jpg` (2000 × 1507 pixels; 739,447 bytes / about 722 KiB).
- Collection: The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York. Accession **37.17.13**.
- Credit: Gift of Edward W. Root, Elihu Root Jr. and Mrs. Ulysses S. Grant III, 1937.
- Rights: **Public domain**, available through The Met's Open Access program.
- [Artwork record and rights information](https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/363420).
- [Original image download](https://images.metmuseum.org/CRDImages/dp/original/DP828309.jpg).
- [Collection API record](https://collectionapi.metmuseum.org/public/collection/v1/objects/363420).

The original 3734 × 2814 download was proportionally resized to 2000 pixels wide and encoded as JPEG for web delivery using macOS `sips` at quality 45. Its full composition is preserved; no creative changes were applied. The engraving is retained as an optional, unused asset and is no longer displayed automatically. The image depicts the Forum as Piranesi saw it in the eighteenth century, rather than a reconstruction of ancient Rome.
